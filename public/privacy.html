Privacy policy

Last updated [date]. Superday Prep is operated by [your name or company] ("we").

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and the name you choose to display.
  • Your practice: which questions you've studied and how you rated them, mock interview and simulation scores, and your readiness history.
  • What you write or upload: interview answers, your resume text, your interview stories, and your networking records. Networking records can include the names, employers and email addresses of people you contact, plus your notes about them.
  • Payments: if you subscribe, our payment processor handles your card. We store only a customer reference and your plan.
  • Usage: the days you used the app and how many AI requests you made, by feature.

How we use it

  • To run the app: save your progress, grade your answers, draft emails and stories, and show your readiness.
  • To bill subscriptions and enforce plan limits.
  • To understand usage in total (for example, how many people ran a simulation this week). We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI features

When you use an AI feature, the text needed for it (for example your answer, your resume or your call notes) is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the response. AI feedback can be wrong. Don't enter information you aren't comfortable sharing for that purpose.

What other people can see

Your progress, resume, stories, contacts and notes are private to you. If you join a club leaderboard, members of that club can see your display name and your counts and scores (points, readiness, streak, questions learned, best simulation score). Nothing else is shared. Club leaders cannot see your answers, resume or notes.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: sign-in and database hosting.
  • Anthropic: AI responses.
  • Stripe: payments.
  • [Hosting provider]: runs the website.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete your account. Deleting your account removes your saved progress, resume, stories, contacts and notes, removes you from any leaderboard and cancels your subscription. Backups held by our providers may persist for a short time afterward.

Your choices

In the app, open your account to download your data or delete your account. For anything else, including questions about this policy, email [support email].

Age

Superday Prep is for university students and is not directed to anyone under [16].

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect.
